## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Where do I see my data?**
A: Open `backend/data.json` - it's all there!

**Q: Can I access from another computer?**
A: Yes! Just use same login credentials.

**Q: Where are my uploaded images?**
A: In `backend/uploads/` folder.

**Q: Will data disappear if I close the app?**
A: No! It's saved in `data.json` permanently.

**Q: Can multiple people use same app?**
A: Yes! Each registers their own account.
